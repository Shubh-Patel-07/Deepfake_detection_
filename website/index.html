Project Overview

Combating Synthetic Misinformation

Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) and Autoencoders allow adversaries to forge realistic face-swapped videos. Our system delivers automated forensic verification.

Spatio-Temporal Hybrid

Single-frame classifiers overlook temporal jitter. Our model pairs a convolutional ResNeXt50 backbone with a recurrent LSTM to detect anomalies across consecutive video frames.

Multi-Dataset Generalization

Trained across three globally acknowledged benchmarks: Meta's DFDC (Deepfake Detection Challenge), Celeb-DF v2, and FaceForensics++ to guard against dataset bias.

Explainable AI (CAM)

Features Class Activation Mapping (CAM) to visually highlight manipulated facial boundaries and artifact zones using OpenCV JET heatmaps.

Inference Workflow

End-to-End Forensic Pipeline

How an uploaded video traverses from raw frames to a final binary classification verdict.

1

Video Ingestion

User selects sequence length (10 to 100 frames; standardized at 20 frames).

2

Stride Extraction

OpenCV samples 20 evenly-spaced temporal frames across the video duration.

3

Face Localization

dlib HOG locates facial landmarks, cropping with 35px padding to 112×112 pixels.

4

ResNeXt50 Spatial

Grouped convolution backbone extracts 2048-dim spatial feature vectors per face.

5

LSTM Sequence

LSTM models frame continuity; Softmax outputs REAL or FAKE verdict + confidence score.

Ingestion & Vision Preprocessing Pipeline

Extracts evenly-distributed frames across video duration to avoid temporal bias.

Locates 68-point facial coordinates with aspect-ratio preserved fallback.

112×112 Normalization

Padding (+35px), bilinear interpolation resize, and ImageNet channel standardization.

Tier 03 • Core AI

Hybrid Spatio-Temporal Neural Architecture

Spatial Feature Extractor

ResNeXt50 (32×4d)

Pre-trained ImageNet backbone truncated at Conv5_x. 32 grouped convolution paths yield 2048-dim spatial representations per frame.

➔
Temporal Sequence Modeling

Unidirectional LSTM

2048 hidden units model frame transitions, tracking unnatural eye-blink pauses, lighting flicker, and face-swap boundary seams.

➔
Classification & Heatmaps

Linear Head + CAM

Dropout(0.4) → Linear(2048, 2) Softmax binary output (Real=1, Fake=0) with Class Activation Mapping (CAM) explainability.
