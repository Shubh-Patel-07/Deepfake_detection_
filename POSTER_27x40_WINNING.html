1. THE DEEPFAKE PROBLEM

Deepfakes utilize Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) and diffusion models to synthesize hyper-realistic facial impersonations in digital video feeds.

Standard single-frame CNNs analyze isolated stills, ignoring vital inter-frame temporal continuity such as blinking frequency, optical flow, and light jitter. By correlating facial landmark trajectory with optical flow anomalies across time, our system exposes synthetic artifacts that elude static inspection.

  • Inter-Frame Flickering: Frame-independent GAN synthesis produces erratic micro-jitter across consecutive frames.
  • Boundary Blending Seams: Face-swap blending edges introduce high-frequency pixel anomalies around the jawline.
