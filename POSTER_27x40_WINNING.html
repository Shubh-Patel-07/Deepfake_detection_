1. THE DEEPFAKE PROBLEM

Deepfakes utilize Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) and diffusion models to synthesize hyper-realistic facial impersonations in video feeds.

Standard single-frame CNNs analyze isolated stills, ignoring vital inter-frame temporal continuity such as blinking frequency, optical flow, and light jitter. This work delivers a Spatio-Temporal Hybrid Framework for reliable, explainable video verification.

  • Spatio-Temporal Fusion: Couple ResNeXt-50 grouped convolutions with LSTM recurrent memory cells to capture micro-flicker anomalies.

  • Benchmark Corpus: Validated on 23,186 video sequences across DFDC (Meta AI), Celeb-DF v2, and FaceForensics++ benchmarks.
