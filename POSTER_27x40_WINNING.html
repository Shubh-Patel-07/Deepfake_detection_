1. THE DEEPFAKE PROBLEM

Deepfakes utilize Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) and diffusion synthesis to replace human facial identities in videos with alarming sensory realism.

Conventional single-frame CNNs analyze isolated stills, failing to detect critical inter-frame temporal discontinuities such as unnatural eye blink intervals, facial jitter, and illumination phase drift. This project engineers a complete Spatio-Temporal Hybrid Framework to deliver robust, explainable video verification.
